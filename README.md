# Alejandro Garbi

Fullstack software engineer based in Calgary. I design, implement, and operate web applications and the services behind them, with a focus on TypeScript, Node.js, GraphQL, and AWS.

I prefer systems that remain maintainable after they ship: explicit data models, typed APIs, indexes that match production access patterns, and infrastructure that can be reproduced. Frontend and backend are treated as one design problem, not two separate deliveries.

## Experience

I hold a Computer Engineering degree from Universidad Simón Bolívar (Venezuela).

At TransaMovil I worked as a fullstack developer and later as team lead on a virtual wallet and e-commerce platform (Node.js, Sails.js, Angular, PostgreSQL, payment integrations). Responsibilities included architecture, implementation, sprint planning, production deployment, and coordination of a five-person engineering team.

At BairesDev I contributed to Vans Customs 3D, a 3D design application for footwear, backpacks, and apparel. I began as a production support engineer (incident response, releases, AWS) and later as a senior backend engineer (Node.js/TypeScript, MySQL, Redis, Terraform), including technical leadership of two engineers.

I currently work in Calgary on product engineering: federated GraphQL APIs, Node.js resolvers, React clients, MongoDB, Redis, and AWS managed with Terraform. Employer details are on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexgarbi10/).
